Jellum — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Developer: Shiri Kadishson Yanay · Contact: 123shirit@gmail.com

Short version: Jellum does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (completed challenges, unlocked fruits, selected fruit) and settings (sound, haptics, translucency) are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • An optional performance test (in test builds only) saves timing numbers on your device. They are never sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Jellum never sees your payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit which packs you own so it can unlock them. We do not keep purchase records on any server. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Network

The only network activity is Apple's StoreKit (product information, purchases, restoring purchases). Everything else works offline.

Children

Jellum is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple's parental controls (such as Ask to Buy) where enabled.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. Jellum will not start collecting data without updating this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
