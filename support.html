Contact: 123shirit@gmail.com · We aim to reply within 3 business days.

I bought a pack but it's locked. Open Collection (grid button) → *Restore purchases*. Packs are tied to the Apple Account that bought them. With Family Sharing, shared packs appear for family members too.

Ask to Buy. The pack shows "Waiting for approval" and unlocks automatically once approved.

Refunds. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded pack is removed from the app automatically.

No sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch (Jellum follows it), the volume, and the speaker button in the top corner. Jellum mixes with music you are playing.

No vibration. Haptics need a supported iPhone and can be turned off in Jellum's Settings or in iOS Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

Progress. Progress is stored only on this device. It is not transferred to a new device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Accessibility. VoiceOver: select the jelly and use the Actions rotor (Squish, Poke, Cut in half). Reduce Motion shortens wobble. Settings → "Less see-through jelly" increases contrast.
